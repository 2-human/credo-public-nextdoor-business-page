Crēdo LegalBusiness postScam alert
Getting calls about a debt, and something feels off? A few signs it may be a scam rather than a real collector: they push you to pay right now with gift cards, crypto, or a wire; they will not give you a company name and mailing address; or they threaten arrest. A legitimate collector has to send written notice, and if you ask, prove the debt is actually yours. When something feels wrong, do not pay on the spot. Ask for it in writing, then check it.
